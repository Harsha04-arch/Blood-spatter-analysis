Bloodstain pattern analysis

Trace every stain back to its origin.

DROP POINT turns crime scene photographs into classified patterns, measured droplets, and an estimated point of origin in two and three dimensions.

See what it doesWorkstation

For academic and research use. Every result is a computational estimate that a qualified forensic professional must validate.

Four analyses, one case file

Upload the scene photographs once. Each analysis builds on the one before it.

  1. Classification

    Names the spatter type (passive drip, transfer smear, medium or high velocity impact) and estimates the impact velocity class.

  2. Droplet analysis

    Isolates every stain and measures its centroid, axes, area and aspect ratio. Impact angle follows from width over length.

  3. 2D convergence

    Traces elongated stains back along their long axis. Where the lines meet is the area of convergence, reported with its RMS error.

  4. 3D point of origin

    Combines each stain's direction and impact angle into rays, then solves for the point in space they pass closest to.
